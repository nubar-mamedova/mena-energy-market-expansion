# MENA Solar Investment Prioritization

**Status:** In progress · May 2026
**Author:** Nubar Mamedova · [LinkedIn](https://linkedin.com/in/nubar-mamedova)

## Project goal
Identify priority MENA markets for utility-scale solar investment using a multi-criteria scoring model built on public data. The deliverable is a ranked recommendation supported by an interactive dashboard and a one-page executive memo.

## Countries analyzed
Six markets selected for geographic and economic diversity across MENA:

- **Morocco** — established regional solar leader (Noor Ouarzazate)
- **Egypt** — large, high-growth market (Benban Solar Park)
- **Saudi Arabia** — giga-scale investment (Vision 2030, NEOM)
- **United Arab Emirates** — record-low solar auction prices, pro-business regulation
- **Jordan** — energy-import-dependent, strategic small market
- **Turkey** — large industrial economy with significant solar manufacturing capacity

## Methodology
A multi-criteria scoring model evaluates each country across four dimensions:

1. **Solar resource quality** — irradiance and solar potential
2. **Market demand** — GDP per capita, electricity consumption, urbanization, demand growth
3. **Policy and regulatory environment** — renewable energy targets, auction frameworks, political stability
4. **Existing renewable infrastructure** — installed capacity, pipeline projects, renewable share of generation

Each country receives a weighted score across these dimensions; the resulting ranking informs the investment prioritization recommendation.
